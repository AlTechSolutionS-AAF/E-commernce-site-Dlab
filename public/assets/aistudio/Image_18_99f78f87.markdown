## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-contrast, dependable dark execution tailored for an exclusive luxury e-commerce experience. The aesthetic merges the sophisticated atmosphere of nocturnal luxury with systematic, utilitarian precision. 

### Brand Personality
- **Sophisticated:** Imbued with the quiet confidence of high-end perfumery and after-dark allure.
- **Uncompromising:** Sharp, structured, and deliberate in every visual interaction.
- **Utilitarian Elegance:** Stripping away decorative excess in favor of a clean, highly legible framework that lets the product take center stage.

### Design Style
Rooted in a strict high-contrast dark execution, the layout utilizes deep obsidian backgrounds punctuated by luminous metallic accents and razor-sharp typographic hierarchies. Generous whitespace and structural discipline create an immersive, boutique-style digital storefront.

## Colors

The color palette is built on a foundation of absolute dark tones, creating a dramatic canvas that accentuates the rich amber and gold hues of luxury formulations.

- **Primary (`#0F0E0D`):** The core background tone, wrapping the viewport in deep, absorbing black.
- **Secondary (`#D4AF37`):** A striking neon gold used selectively for focal points, active states, and high-value call-to-actions.
- **Neutral Surface (`#1E1C1A`):** Used for cards, elevated containers, and structural dividers to provide subtle tonal separation.
- **Text (`#FFFFFF`):** High-contrast pure white for optimal legibility and stark luxury editorial styling.

## Typography

The typography system relies exclusively on `Inter` to deliver a clean, universally understood, and utilitarian e-commerce layout. By strictly varying weight, size, and tracking rather than mixing font families, the interface achieves a modern, architectural rhythm.

### Scale & Hierarchy
- Headlines command attention through measured tracking and bold weights, avoiding superfluous ornamentation.
- Body text remains neutral, highly functional, and optimized for dense product descriptions and editorial storytelling.
- Labels utilize uppercase tracking to evoke high-end product labeling and minimalist packaging design.

## Layout & Spacing

A disciplined fluid grid system governs the layout, prioritizing breathing room and deliberate product isolation. 

### Spacing Rhythm
- **Gutters & Margins:** Generous outer margins (`32px` base) frame the viewport, while `24px` grid gutters maintain structural alignment across collections.
- **Component Scale:** The `space-*` scale ensures predictable spatial intervals, preventing visual clutter and emphasizing individual product cards.
- **Responsive Adaptations:** On mobile viewports, margins scale down to `16px` while maintaining consistent vertical rhythm between editorial blocks.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is articulated through subtle tonal layering and low-contrast outlines rather than heavy drop shadows. 

- **Surface Tiers:** The deep obsidian background (`#0F0E0D`) serves as the base layer, with elevated containers rendered in `#1E1C1A` to create a restrained, matte dimensionality.
- **Ghost Borders:** Interactive elements and cards utilize razor-thin, low-opacity white or gold outlines to delineate boundaries without breaking the minimalist dark aesthetic.

## Shapes

Adhering strictly to a refined, architectural edge profile (`roundedness: 1`), UI elements feature crisp `0.25rem` corners. This slight softening prevents visual harshness while preserving the razor-sharp, high-end industrial design language appropriate for luxury cosmetics and fragrance packaging.

## Components

Every component reflects the systematic, high-contrast dark execution of the visual identity.

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Neon gold (`#D4AF37`) background with high-contrast dark text, sharp edges (`0.25rem` roundedness), and subtle hover state luminosity.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background with a `1px` solid white or gold outline, shifting to a solid neutral surface on hover.

### Chips & Tags
- Compact, pill-adjacent or strictly squared tags featuring a `1px` border, used for categorizing fragrance notes (e.g., *Woody*, *Amber*, *Musk*).

### Input Fields
- Dark surface backgrounds (`#1E1C1A`) with clean, monoline borders. Focus states transition instantly to neon gold, reinforcing precision and interactivity.

### Cards
- Product cards utilize the surface-dark background with zero elevation shadows, relying entirely on framing, high-resolution photography, and meticulous typography to convey luxury.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Custom-rendered geometric toggles featuring crisp borders, utilizing solid neon gold fills for selected states.
